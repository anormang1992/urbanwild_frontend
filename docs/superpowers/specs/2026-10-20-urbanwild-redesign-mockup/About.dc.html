About UrbanWild

It started with a turtle.

Susan and Rex Ballard have been television producers for more than three decades. About ten years ago, they turned their cameras on the people who give injured wildlife a second chance.

It all started when Susan picked up a turtle in the middle of the road that had been hit by a car. She rushed it to a local licensed wildlife rehabilitator. Once she learned how many North American wildlife species are given a second chance at life in wildlife clinics, hospitals and sanctuaries, her world would never be the same.

She rushed home and asked Rex to grab his video camera. There were lots of wildlife stories out there, just waiting to be told.

“Visual education would be paramount to protecting the wildlife around us.”

Soon Rex and Susan were filming all over North Carolina with federally and state licensed wildlife rehabilitators. A sad reality quickly became clear: most wildlife injuries are the result of human interaction, one way or another. Helping people understand the animals around them became the mission.
